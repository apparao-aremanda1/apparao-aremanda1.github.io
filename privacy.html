Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Overview

PingScout ("we", "our", or "us") provides automated data processing, web scraping, and notification services via WhatsApp, Email, and Web APIs. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our website or automated messaging bots.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to deliver requested notifications and custom automation services:

  • WhatsApp Interactions: Your WhatsApp phone number and incoming message text when you send a command or query to our bot.
  • Project Intake Forms: Your name, email address, and project requirements submitted via our request form.
  • Technical Logs: Automated system logs containing timestamps and request statuses for debugging and service maintenance.

3. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Deliver requested automated summaries, notifications, and document alerts.
  • Respond to your custom software requests and send technical project quotes.
  • Ensure operational security and improve the efficiency of our automated systems.

4. Data Sharing & Privacy Safeguards

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or phone numbers to any third parties under any circumstances.

Data is only processed through encrypted APIs required to deliver the service (e.g., Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for message routing).

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can request complete removal of your phone number or data from our database at any time by messaging "STOP" or "DELETE" directly to our WhatsApp bot, or by emailing our administrator.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us via our website at pingscout.ai.
